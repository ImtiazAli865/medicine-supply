**Goal**: The supplier records a cash payment with a preview of the balance after it and how it is allocated; every issued invoice shows unpaid, partly paid or paid, worked out from the entries; an overpayment is kept as an advance and settles the next invoice; a payment never reaches another store.

**Independent Test**: Balance 0. Issue 20,000, pay 15,000: balance 5,000 and "Partly paid". Issue 8,000: 13,000. Pay 10,000: 3,000, the first invoice "Paid", the second "Partly paid".
