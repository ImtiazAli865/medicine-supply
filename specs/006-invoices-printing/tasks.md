## Implementation Strategy

1. **MVP first**: Phases 1 and 2, then US1 only. Stop, run its tests, apply the migration to the TEST project (with the owner's say-so), and open a draft with real orders. This alone shows the supplier exactly what will be billed, with no number used.
2. **Then US2** (issue), because it makes the invoice a real, frozen record, then US3 (print), US4 (cancel and replace), US5 (list and Home card) and US6 (order sheets).
3. **Do not claim anything unrun.** Record real counts only; investigate any intermittent failure; the owner's phone check (T064) and the ADR consent (T065) are not skipped; the prints are checked with print-media emulation and PDF text, not a real printer, and the quickstart says so.
4. **Keep the rules in the database.** If a rule matters for integrity (one live invoice per order, exactly-once number, no cost, immutability, business isolation, the totals rule, the order moving to fulfilled), it is enforced in a function, constraint or trigger and tested there, not only in the screen.
5. **Use the Module 004 and 005 lessons**: write the read queries with materialized CTEs, measure at 1,000 invoices and 60 lines (T057), and fix any slowness with a new migration rather than editing `0007`; compare existing row counts before and after applying a migration that touches older objects (T015, T063).
6. **Module 007 hooks, not code**: do not add an Account Summary, a payment field or paid statuses; keep the invoice tables ready (a later module adds its own tables and reads issued invoices only).
