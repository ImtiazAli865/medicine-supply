## Implementation Strategy

1. **MVP first**: Phases 1 and 2, then US1 only. Stop, run its tests, apply the migration to the test project, and open the list with real orders. This alone replaces the handwritten, hand-added market list.
2. **Then US2** (purchase recording), because it turns the list into a working tool at the market, then US3 (print and CSV) and US4 (Home card, search and filters).
3. **Do not claim anything unrun.** Record real counts only; investigate any intermittent failure; the owner's phone check (T057) and the ADR consent (T058) are not skipped; the print is checked with print-media emulation and a PDF, not a real printer, and the quickstart says so.
4. **Keep the rules in the database.** If a rule matters for integrity (append-only entries, exactly-once saves, no cost in the list, business isolation, the sum rule), it is enforced in the function or a trigger and tested there, not only in the screen.
5. **Use the Module 004 lesson**: write the read queries with materialized CTEs, measure at 1,000 lines (T051), and fix any slowness with a new migration rather than editing `0006`.
