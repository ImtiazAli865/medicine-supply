Module 005 Market list and purchases: the consolidated wholesale market list and purchase recording for the Medicine Supply & Order Management app (builds on Module 001 products/stores, Module 002 import, Module 003 manual orders and Module 004 pending products, all merged into master; the order tables, the order price snapshot rules and ADR-0003/ADR-0004 stay as they are). Scope ONLY: (1) Consolidated market list screen: for a selected business date (shown and filtered in Pakistan Standard Time, default today) it adds up the quantities of all CONFIRMED, not cancelled orders confirmed on that date (draft and cancelled orders never count). Each medicine is grouped by its product ID, never by similar text, so different strengths, dosage forms, manufacturers or pack sizes are never combined. Each row shows the medicine name with strength/form/pack size and unit, the total requested quantity, the quantity requested by each store (store name and quantity), the purchased quantity, the remaining quantity to purchase, and a purchase status (Not bought, Partly bought, Fully bought, Bought extra). Pending lines (typed medicines not yet registered) MUST appear in a separate 'Not yet registered' group, grouped by the exact typed name (compared ignoring case and repeated spaces) with a store-wise breakdown, so no requested quantity is ever left out; the sum of all quantities on the list must equal the sum of the quantities of the confirmed order lines of that date. Search by medicine name, filter by status (all, still to buy, bought) and by store, and select the date. The list reflects the current orders: when an order is confirmed, cancelled, has a quantity edited, or a typed line is registered or linked (Module 004), the list changes accordingly without losing purchases already recorded; a registered line moves from 'Not yet registered' into the normal grouping. (2) Purchase recording: from a row the supplier records an actual purchase entry: purchased quantity (whole number 1 to 100000, required), actual unit cost in PKR with two decimals (optional, because the real cost is often known only at the market), supplier or market name (optional text), purchase reference (optional text), and the purchase date (default the list date). Several entries per medicine per date are allowed, so partial purchases and later top-ups work. Remaining = requested minus the sum of purchased quantities, never below zero; buying more than requested is allowed and shown as 'Bought extra N'. A checkbox or 'mark bought' control MUST NOT claim a full purchase by itself: the only way to mark something bought is to record a quantity, and a quick action 'Bought all remaining' may pre-fill the form with the remaining quantity but still needs the supplier's confirmation and records that exact quantity. Purchase entries are append-only: a wrong entry is cancelled (voided) with a reason and a corrected entry is added, the history of entries (who, when, quantity, cost, voided or not) stays visible, and nothing is silently overwritten or deleted. A retried save creates exactly one entry (client-generated request id). Pending (unregistered) groups cannot receive a purchase entry until the medicine is registered or linked through Module 004; the row shows a 'Register this medicine' link instead, and the quantity stays in the list. After a purchase with an actual unit cost the app MAY offer, only after the supplier confirms, to update the medicine's current Purchase Price from that cost; it never changes a price silently and never touches order line price snapshots. Purchased quantity is a separate number from ordered quantity and from delivered quantity: recording a purchase never changes an order, an order line or a delivered quantity. (3) Printing and export of the market list: a print layout for the consolidated market list (compact, suitable for taking to the market, each medicine with its total and its store-wise breakdown, the 'Not yet registered' group included and marked 'unregistered', optionally limited to selected stores, business name and date in the header, purchased and remaining columns left for hand writing or filled from recorded purchases) and a CSV export of the same rows. Purchase Price, actual unit cost, MRP, margin and profit are NEVER printed or exported on the market list. The individual-store order print and the combined store-wise order print (master prompt Section 10 options A and B) belong to Module 006 and are not part of this module. (4) Visibility: a link and a count ('Market list: N medicines to buy today') on the Home screen and from the orders area; empty state with the next action ('No confirmed orders for this date. Take or confirm an order.') on the market list and on the purchase history; loading and error states with Retry; mobile-first English UI, large touch targets (at least 44 px), no sideways scroll at 360 px; the bottom navigation keeps five items. Success criteria must be testable, including: the master prompt example (Al Madina 20, Shah Medical 10, Rehman Medical 15 Panadol gives total 45 with the three store quantities); different strengths of the same name stay separate rows; the sum of the list quantities equals the sum of the confirmed order line quantities for the date, pending lines included; cancelled and draft orders never counted; a quantity edit or cancellation changes the list; partial purchases (for example 20 of 45 then 10 more) give purchased 30 and remaining 15; buying more than requested shows bought extra; a retried purchase save, including 10 simultaneous identical requests, creates exactly one entry; a voided entry stops counting and stays in the history with its reason; recording a purchase changes no order, order line, delivered quantity or price snapshot; no cost, MRP or profit in the print or export; two businesses are isolated by RLS for every new table and function; a 360 px layout passes the touch-target check; the list for 200 orders and 1,000 lines opens within 3 seconds on the test project. OUT OF SCOPE: invoices, invoice numbers, invoice printing and the order print options A and B (Module 006), ledger and payments (Module 007), voice and Gemini (Module 008), offline sync and offline purchase recording (Module 009), profit and reports built from purchase costs (Module 010; this module only records the cost), splitting a purchased quantity between stores or changing delivered quantities, supplier/vendor master data, multi-user roles, purchase returns. Follow docs/master-prompt.txt (sections 7A, 8E purchases, 9 consolidated market list, 10 option C, 16 screens 7 and 8, 17 items 8 to 11 and 23) and docs/negative-prompt.txt (never leave an unregistered line out of the market list, never omit the store breakdown, never treat a purchase checkbox as proof of purchase without a recorded quantity, never group by similar text, never show purchase price on customer-facing output) and .specify/memory/constitution.md principles I-VIII. ADR-0003 and ADR-0004 document the order and pending write models; purchase writes must follow the same pattern (SECURITY DEFINER function, replay-safe request id, append-only history, business isolation).
